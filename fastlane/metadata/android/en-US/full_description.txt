Turn "argue over a list" into a game of this or that.

Lilt ranks names by rapid pairwise choice. For two people it reveals where you already agree — but only once you have both done the work, so nobody is anchored by the other's answers.

• Fast pairwise ranking — one tap per choice
• A peeking lock, so you cannot see their list before you have made your own
• Mutual-match reveal: the names you BOTH ranked high
• A real ranking engine underneath, not a running tally

No accounts, no cloud, no ads. The APK asks for no network permission at all.

Free and open source, MIT licensed.
